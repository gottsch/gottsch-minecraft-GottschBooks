Every tool comes in five materials: *wood*, *stone*, *iron*, *gold* and *diamond*. Better materials dig faster and
last longer. Gold is the fastest of all, and breaks almost at once.

## The Crafting Table {#table}

```recipe minecraft:crafting_table
Four planks of any wood. Every other tool starts here.
```
