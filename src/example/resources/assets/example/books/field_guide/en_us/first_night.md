The sun sets about **ten minutes** after you arrive. Before it does, you need three things:

1. **Wood**, from any tree. Punch the trunk.
2. **A shelter.** A hole in a hillside will do.
3. **Light.** Coal and sticks make torches.

When night falls, {dark_red}zombies{} and {dark_red}skeletons{} come out. *Stay inside.* Creepers make no sound
until it's too late.

> Tip: a bed skips the night, but only if you sleep in it before the monsters find you.

## The Torch {#torch}

```spotlight minecraft:torch
Light stops monsters spawning nearby. Place them **everywhere**.
```
