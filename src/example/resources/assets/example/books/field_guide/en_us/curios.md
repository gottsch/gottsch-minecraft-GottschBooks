Many items look alike until you know what is in them. A potion's effect, a book's enchantment and the colour of dyed
leather are all kept on the item itself.

![A Potion of Healing](item:minecraft:potion{Potion:"minecraft:healing"})

Brewed from an awkward potion and a glistering melon slice. Hover over it to see what it does.

## Enchanted Books {#books}

```spotlight minecraft:enchanted_book{StoredEnchantments:[{id:"minecraft:mending",lvl:1s}]}
Found in chests, fished up, or traded from a librarian. Combine it with a tool in an anvil.
```

## Dyed Leather {#leather}

```spotlight minecraft:leather_chestplate{display:{color:11546150}}
Craft leather armour with any dye to colour it.
```

## Pictures in Text {#fonts}

A resource pack font can draw pictures in a line of text: {white}{font:example:icons}d e g r{} are diamond,
emerald, gold and redstone, from the example pack's icons font. In {font:minecraft:alt}the enchanting
table's script{}, a font changes the letters themselves.
