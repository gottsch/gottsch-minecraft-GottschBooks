Many items look alike until you know what is in them. A potion's effect, a book's enchantment and the colour of dyed
leather are all kept on the item itself.

![A Potion of Healing](item:minecraft:potion[potion_contents={potion:"minecraft:healing"}])

Brewed from an awkward potion and a glistering melon slice. Hover over it to see what it does.

## Enchanted Books {#books}

```spotlight minecraft:enchanted_book[stored_enchantments={levels:{"minecraft:mending":1}}]
Found in chests, fished up, or traded from a librarian. Combine it with a tool in an anvil.
```

## Dyed Leather {#leather}

```spotlight minecraft:leather_chestplate[dyed_color={rgb:11546150}]
Craft leather armour with any dye to colour it.
```
