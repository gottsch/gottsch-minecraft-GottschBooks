Every ore has its depth. Dig too high and you find only coal and copper; dig too deep and the stone turns to
deepslate, slower to mine but richer.

A few rules of thumb:

- Branch mines are cheapest at a single depth: pick the ore you want most.
- Caves show more ore than mining does, but bring torches and armour.
- Mark your way back. Every tunnel looks the same after an hour.

## Where to Dig {#where}

| Ore | Found | Most common at | Mine with |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| ![](item:minecraft:coal_ore) Coal | almost everywhere above y 0 | y 96 | ![](item:minecraft:wooden_pickaxe) any pickaxe |
| ![](item:minecraft:copper_ore) Copper | y -16 to 112 | y 48 | ![](item:minecraft:stone_pickaxe) stone or better |
| ![](item:minecraft:iron_ore) ![](item:minecraft:deepslate_iron_ore) Iron | nearly every depth | y 16 | ![](item:minecraft:stone_pickaxe) stone or better |
| ![](item:minecraft:gold_ore) Gold | y -64 to 32 | y -16 | ![](item:minecraft:iron_pickaxe) iron or better |
| ![](item:minecraft:redstone_ore) Redstone | y -64 to 15 | y -59 | ![](item:minecraft:iron_pickaxe) iron or better |
| ![](item:minecraft:diamond_ore) ![](item:minecraft:deepslate_diamond_ore) **Diamond** | y -64 to 16 | y -59 | ![](item:minecraft:iron_pickaxe) iron or better, see [Finding Diamonds](entry:diamonds) |

> Deepslate versions of each ore replace the stone ones below y 0, and drop the same thing.
