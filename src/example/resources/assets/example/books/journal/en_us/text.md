---
title: The Lighthouse Keeper's Journal
author: Old Maren
---

*First night of the autumn storms.*

The lamp took three tries to light. The wick is damp, and so am I. Two ships passed close to the rocks before
midnight; both saw the light and turned. That is the whole of the job, and tonight it was enough.

![](item:minecraft:spyglass)

---

*Second night.*

A crate washed up below the cliff. Inside: salt fish, a coil of rope, and one {dark_aqua}prismarine shard{}, still
wet and humming faintly when held to the ear.

![A gift from the deep](item:minecraft:prismarine_shard)

I have put it on the windowsill, facing the sea. **It is not mine to keep.**

---

*Third night.*

Something knocked at the door at the turn of the tide. I did not open it. In the morning there were footprints in
the sand: they came up from the water, and they went back down to it.

The shard is gone from the windowsill.

---

*Fourth night.*

The storm has passed. The sea is flat and grey, and the lamp lit on the first try. I have written up the tides for
the week, as the keeper before me did:

| Day | High water | Low water |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Monday | before dawn | mid-morning |
| Tuesday | dawn | noon |
| Wednesday | mid-morning | early afternoon |
| Thursday | noon | {dark_aqua}dusk{} |

I think we have an understanding, the sea and I.

---

*Fifth night.*

A bottle on the doorstep at dawn, stoppered with kelp.

```spotlight minecraft:potion{Potion:"minecraft:water_breathing"}
It smells of salt. I have not drunk it. Not yet.
```

I drew the view from the lamp room tonight, so that I would remember it.
