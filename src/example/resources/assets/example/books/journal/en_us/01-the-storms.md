# The Storms

*First night of the autumn storms.*

The lamp took three tries to light. The wick is damp, and so am I. Two ships passed close to the rocks before
midnight; both saw the light and turned. That is the whole of the job, and tonight it was enough.

![](item:minecraft:spyglass)

---

*Second night.*

A crate washed up below the cliff. Inside: salt fish, a coil of rope, and one {dark_aqua}prismarine shard{}, still
wet and humming faintly when held to the ear.

![A gift from the deep](item:minecraft:prismarine_shard)

I have put it on the windowsill, facing the sea. **It is not mine to keep.**
